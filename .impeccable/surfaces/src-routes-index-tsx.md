# Página inicial (landing)

Scope: the public landing page at / in its complete version, prototyped in design/prototypes/inicio-monte-na-hora.html. The live route changes only after the user approves. Visitor mode: Persuade. The page inherits the PAVOX world (Extrato plus the binding brand: logo files, navy #001848, PAVOX blue #0055fb, pale-blue highlighter, Bricolage Grotesque with Hanken Grotesk, ruled statement rows). The phone mock shows the buyer checkout in its own Comprovante world.

Audience and action: a Brazilian seller of digital or physical products, often running paid traffic, comparing checkouts. The primary action is "Começar grátis" on the Free plan. Proof: none yet (no clients, numbers, or testimonials). The page proves itself by the product working in front of the visitor. Real facts only: plans Free R$ 0 / 1,99%, Growth R$ 29,90 / 1,49%, Pro R$ 99 / 0,99%; the supported gateways; card through Mercado Pago only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing lets the visitor build a real checkout in the first viewport and watch a sale land. It refuses the stock hero (headline, screenshot, logo strip) and the feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: The first viewport is drenched in PAVOX navy, with the white-and-blue logo variant. Everything after it sits on statement-white with navy headings and navy section rules. Content is ruled rows and columns, never cards. PAVOX blue carries actions. The pale-blue highlighter marks one thing per section at most. The phone mock renders the Comprovante checkout in the chosen lojista color.

STORY: The visitor types a product and price, picks a color, and sees the checkout ready. They tap Pix and see it confirmed, with the sale appearing as a line in the lojista statement. They then learn where the money lands (their own gateway), what the page offers for digital and physical products, and what it costs, down to the real fee for their volume. They start free.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop has the headline, the lede and four fact rows on the left, over navy: Pix, boleto e cartão; 12 gateways; digital ou físico; Plano Free. The "Criar conta grátis" CTA sits under them. On the right a phone plays an example checkout by itself: it fills the fields, pays by Pix, shows the PAGO stamp, and the sale lands in the statement strip. Example chips switch between Curso and Produto físico (the latter in the lojista's pink to show store color), with a pause control. Mobile has the headline, the phone, then the facts and the CTA. A "Comece em 3 passos" section follows the hero.

FORM: "Checkout de verdade". The user replaced the earlier "Monte na hora" builder, asking for "Informações, a opção 1 e outras coisas": an autoplaying example checkout, fact rows, and a 3-step start section. It keeps the same signature (Pix paid, the PAGO stamp, the sale line sliding into the statement strip). Autoplay pauses offscreen and does not start under prefers-reduced-motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
